About GamingTube

GamingTube is a student-built directory platform that helps gamers discover amazing YouTube channels, videos, and the games they cover.

What We Offer

  • ✅ Browse hundreds of gaming channels
  • ✅ Discover the latest videos and games
  • ✅ Read and leave reviews
  • ✅ Channel owners can register and add their content
  • ✅ Built with Django + Bootstrap

Our Mission

We created GamingTube as part of the CSC1025 module to demonstrate clean Django development, class-based views, user authentication, image uploads, and more.
